# Completed menu-confirmation cohort

The live investigation added focus-based confirmation at **Get In Car** and **Upgrades & Tuning**, retaining existing arrow pacing and transaction checks. Those additional labels have not been promoted to the public default.

The full funded batch completed **33 cars in 2,012 seconds (33m 32s)**, including recovery and stopped-worker time. Its median recorded active cycle was **53.718 seconds**, compared with **53.797 seconds** in the preceding comparison sample. Three failed attempts were recorded. This does not establish a meaningful overall speed improvement: the cohorts were sequential and conditions were not controlled.

One recovery exposed a private startup predicate that rejected a receipt-confirmed partial mastery checkpoint. The corrected resume continued the paid car, skipped its already-owned node, completed the reward, and only then bought another car. That private startup predicate is not part of the public runtime.

Cleanup was deferred under the final-only policy. Farming and final cleanup are outside the conversion duration above, so this result cannot establish saved Super Wheelspins per hour. All recovery time remains part of the full-operation measurement.

[Aggregate data](menu-confirmation-cohort-20260926.json) contains no account identifiers, receipts, screenshots or credentials.
